**Definitions (to confirm with the science team):** *image* = one detector of one exposure; *exposure* = one `obs_id`: all its detectors are read at the same time, so it is **one epoch**. SPHEREx's two arrays see the same sky through a beam splitter (D1+D4, D2+D5, D3+D6), so a point usually lands on 2 images per exposure; *visit* = exposures less than 30 days apart (SPHEREx returns to each point about every 6 months, and stays on it for days to weeks). For blinking: exposures hours apart (moving objects), visits months apart (proper motion, variability).
